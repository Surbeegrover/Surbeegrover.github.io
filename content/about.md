I'm Surbee. I love taking gen AI from a cool demo to something that does real work: agents with real jobs, specs that keep AI-built code honest, and small tools that save people time.

This is where I share what I build, what I learn, and what didn't work, as I go. Some posts are finished thoughts. Others are notes from work in progress.

## What I write about

- **Agents and tooling:** building agents, MCP servers, and the glue around them
- **Spec-driven development:** what changes when the spec is the source of truth
- **AI across the software lifecycle:** planning, building, testing, reviewing, operating
- **Governance:** human review, audit trails, and evals for AI-built software
- **Learning notes:** certifications, courses, and experiments

## Ground rules

- I write about methods and lessons, not about clients or employers.
- Everything here is my own view, not anyone else's.
- If something I wrote turns out to be wrong, I'll correct it in the open.
